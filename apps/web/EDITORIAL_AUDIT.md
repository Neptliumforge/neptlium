# Neptlium public editorial authority

The public Web experience presents Neptlium as capital operating infrastructure and preserves a strict distinction between public explanation, analytical or modeled context, and privileged financial authority.

Canonical public discovery is Platform, Products, Solutions, Resources, and Company. Primary product discovery is Capital Account, Treasury, Allocation, and Portfolio Intelligence. Performance and Capital Universe remain valid supporting product routes. Press remains a company/footer surface rather than a primary navigation destination.

Public copy must not imply that Neptlium is a bank, broker, custodian, investment adviser, payment institution, settlement provider, or direct asset manager unless repository authority and verified company status explicitly establish such a role. Public copy must not manufacture customer scale, assets under management, performance, uptime, certifications, integrations, regulatory status, or provider capability.

The canonical conceptual system is: capital state -> Neptlium operating context -> governed work. Financial information should preserve distinctions among observed, provider-reported, derived, modeled, proposed, reviewed, authorized, pending, completed, failed, settled, and reconciled state where those concepts are supported. No public surface should collapse uncertainty into certainty or workflow state into financial consequence.

Homepage primary product entry appears only at the header, hero, and final authority section in the rendered architecture. Category discovery uses text links or quiet row actions rather than competing conversion buttons.
