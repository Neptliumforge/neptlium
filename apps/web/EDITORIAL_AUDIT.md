# Neptlium public editorial authority

The public Web experience presents Neptlium as capital operating infrastructure and preserves a strict distinction between public explanation, analytical or modeled context, and privileged financial authority.

The canonical public backbone is:

1. `Capital operating infrastructure.`
2. `The operating system for capital.`
3. `Capital should remain intelligible as it moves.`
4. `Clarity before consequence.`

The canonical operating model is `Capital State -> Operating Context -> Governed Work`. Public explanation should reinforce that model rather than present Neptlium as a generic intelligence dashboard, trading terminal, bank, broker, custodian, or asset manager.

Canonical public discovery is Platform, Products, Solutions, Resources, and Company. Primary product discovery is Capital Account, Treasury, Allocation, and Portfolio Intelligence. Performance and Capital Universe remain valid supporting product routes. Press remains a company/footer surface rather than a primary navigation destination.

Public copy must not imply that Neptlium is a bank, broker, custodian, investment adviser, payment institution, settlement provider, or direct asset manager unless repository authority and verified company status explicitly establish such a role. Public copy must not manufacture customer scale, assets under management, performance, uptime, certifications, integrations, regulatory status, provider capability, or predictive certainty.

Financial information must preserve distinctions among observed, reported, derived, modeled, proposed, reviewed, authorized, and consequential state where those concepts are supported. Provider submission, observation, review, or authorization must not be described as canonical balance, settlement, or financial consequence unless the underlying state supports that claim.

Homepage primary product entry appears only at the header, hero, and final authority section in the rendered architecture. Category discovery uses text links or quiet row actions rather than competing conversion buttons. The marketing site should explain why the operating model matters before asking the customer to enter the authenticated product.
