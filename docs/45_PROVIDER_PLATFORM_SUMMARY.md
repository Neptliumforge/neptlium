# Neptlium Provider Platform Summary

**Circle:** digital-dollar/stablecoin wallet and settlement infrastructure.

**Alchemy:** multi-chain blockchain connectivity, observation and intelligence infrastructure.

**Stripe:** fiat/card/bank-payment and billing infrastructure.

**Neptlium Platform Core:** identity/ownership, authorization, policy/risk/approvals, transaction intents, canonical ledger, lifecycle, audit and reconciliation.

The platform rule is simple: providers supply capabilities and evidence; Neptlium owns financial authority and truth.

Alchemy is multi-chain by architecture, with each network independently configured/certified. Circle and Stripe externally mutating capabilities are separately certified and execution-gated. Provider configuration, provider health and provider success never bypass Neptlium authorization or reconciliation.
