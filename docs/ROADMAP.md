# Roadmap

Neptlium is developing a crypto-first institutional capital operating platform in controlled stages.

## Current priorities

1. Harden identity, authorization, RLS, auditability, and operational controls.
2. Connect verified custody-provider data to Neptlium Wallet.
3. Complete portfolio, treasury, and allocation read models without fabricated financial data.
4. Build the public institutional site at `apps/web` for `https://neptlium.com`.
5. Add reconciliation, reporting, monitoring, and incident-response workflows.

Execution capabilities remain unavailable until provider authentication, idempotency, reconciliation, authorization, and audit requirements are satisfied.

Roadmap items are directional and do not represent released capabilities.
